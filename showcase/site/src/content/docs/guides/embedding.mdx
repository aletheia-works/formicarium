The showcase terminals use only the public exports of
`@aletheia-works/formicarium`: `createSession` from `/browser`, and
`createCommandSession` and `createInstaller` from the package root (the
installer quietly installs the tools every page gets, such as nano). Your page
can do the same.

## Requirements

- **Same origin.** The Worker (`runtime/web/package-worker.bundle.js`) and the
  core under `assets/paludarium/` (the loader `index.mjs`, the modules next to
  it, `paludarium.wasm` and `build-info.json`) must be served from your page's
  origin. Pass the absolute URLs of the Worker, loader, wasm and build
  information as `assets`; the other modules are read next to the loader.
- **Cross-origin isolation.** Serve COOP `same-origin` and COEP `require-corp`,
  or use a service worker such as coi-serviceworker on static hosting.
- **CSP.** Allow same-origin scripts and Workers and WebAssembly compilation,
  for example `script-src 'self' 'wasm-unsafe-eval'; worker-src 'self';
  connect-src 'self'`. `blob:` is not needed. The core is loaded from the asset
  URLs after its digests are checked, so serve them from an origin you trust.
- **Guests.** Use a catalog (`GuestCatalog`) whose entries name same-origin
  guest files with their SHA256 and `build-info.json`. `install <tool>` checks
  every byte before it registers a guest.

## Output and display

`CommandSession.execute` returns the original `stdout` and `stderr` bytes of
each command. For a guest run they also arrive in chunks through `onOutput`;
write one or the other, not both. Decode the bytes for display and never treat
them as HTML. `onProgress` reports `totalBytes: null` when the total is unknown,
which differs from `0`. Pass an `AbortSignal` to cancel, and dispose the
session when the page goes away.
